## How Accenture describes its hiring {#overview}
source: https://www.accenture.com/us-en/careers/explore-careers/area-of-interest/journey-to-accenture

Accenture's careers FAQ says interviews are held by phone, by video or in person, and that the number of interviews depends on the role. It describes an online technical assessment completed in one sitting within a specified timeframe, typically within 72 hours, and says the assessment is one part of the hiring process and does not replace interviews.

## Behavioral interviews {#behavioral}
tags: behavioral
source: https://www.accenture.com/us-en/blogs/blogs-careers/how-to-prepare-for-a-behavioral-interview

An Accenture careers article from 2022 recommends the STAR method (situation, task, action, result) and preparing one or two significant stories from past major projects. It says Accenture looks for behaviors that demonstrate collaboration, leadership and problem-solving skills, and also lists ability to learn, business acumen and emotional resilience.

## Case-study interviews {#case-study}
roles: consulting, business-analysis
source: https://www.accenture.com/us-en/blogs/blogs-careers/secrets-to-a-successful-case-study-interview

An Accenture careers article on case-study interviews from 2023 says interviewers may spend the first half of the interview on the candidate's previous experience, that what matters most is demonstrating problem solving, and that candidates should explain their thought process and assumptions. It says candidates can ask the interviewer to define an acronym or to repeat or confirm details.
