## How Amazon describes its interviews {#overview}
source: https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/university/additional-tech
source: https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/sde-iii-interview-prep

Amazon's interview prep pages say a significant part of each interview covers how the candidate has demonstrated Amazon's Leadership Principles in past jobs. Each interviewer typically asks two or three behavioral questions about successes or challenges. Amazon suggests answering with the STAR method (situation, task, action, result), says answers should include metrics or data where applicable, and says it does not ask brain teasers.

## Leadership Principles {#leadership-principles}
tags: behavioral, leadership
source: https://www.amazon.jobs/en/principles

Amazon publishes 16 Leadership Principles:
- Customer Obsession
- Ownership
- Invent and Simplify
- Are Right, A Lot
- Learn and Be Curious
- Hire and Develop the Best
- Insist on the Highest Standards
- Think Big
- Bias for Action
- Frugality
- Earn Trust
- Dive Deep
- Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
- Deliver Results
- Strive to be Earth's Best Employer
- Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility

Practice questions can ask for one specific past example that shows one of these principles.

## Preparing for AWS interviews {#aws}
source: https://aws.amazon.com/careers/how-we-hire/

AWS describes four hiring steps: online application, assessments, phone interview and in-person interviews. It advises candidates to consider their own successes and failures in relation to the Leadership Principles before interviewing, and to plan to describe their experiences using the STAR method. AWS interviews include a bar raiser, described as an objective third party who focuses on long-term vision rather than the immediate hiring need.

## Senior engineering and engineering manager screens {#senior-technical-screens}
levels: senior, lead
tags: technical, system-design
source: https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/sde-iii-interview-prep
source: https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/sdm-interview-prep

For senior software development engineer (SDE III) roles, Amazon says the 60-minute technical phone screen is with a senior leader and splits its time evenly between Leadership Principles questions and coding and system design, and that candidates should expect at least one software systems design question. For software development manager roles, the phone screen splits its time between Leadership Principles questions and system design. For both roles, Amazon describes the interview loop as five 55-minute interviews.
