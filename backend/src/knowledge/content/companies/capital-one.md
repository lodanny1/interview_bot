## How Capital One describes its interviews {#overview}
source: https://www.capitalonecareers.com/what-to-expect-during-your-capital-one-interview-students-101

Capital One's description of its general process says candidates who complete the initial application steps are invited to a series of interviews called a Power Day. All candidates have one or two job fit interviews, which assess aptitude for the role, the team and Capital One's culture. Behavioral interviews, with questions that usually begin "Tell me about a time when...", are used for most positions. Some candidates take an hour-long virtual exam on knowledge of the position, and Capital One requires video for all virtual interviews.

## Case interviews {#case-interviews}
roles: business-analysis, product-management
source: https://www.capitalonecareers.com/what-to-expect-during-your-capital-one-interview-students-101
source: https://www.capitalonecareers.com/4-tips-to-ace-your-capital-one-case-interview-101-students

Capital One says campus, business analyst, product manager and finance positions typically require a case or mini-case interview, in which the candidate works through a real-life business problem; a mini-case takes about 30 minutes. Its case interview tips say there is usually more than one right answer, the answer should be a recommendation about what the business should do and why, and candidates should talk through their thinking, defend it with data, break down their approach in non-technical terms, and let their skills show through the case instead of talking about their resume.

## Business analyst interviews {#business-analyst}
roles: business-analysis
source: https://www.capitalonecareers.com/cracking-the-business-analyst-interview-students-101-cdev

Capital One's article on business analyst interviews says they typically start with a mini case that assesses initial problem-solving skills, that candidates should be prepared for a good deal of math and keep track of their numbers, and that interviewers look at how candidates think, approach problems and communicate. It recommends practicing real case examples, even with a friend.

## Data science roles {#data-science}
roles: data-analytics
source: https://www.capitalonecareers.com/what-to-expect-during-your-capital-one-interview-students-101

For data science roles, Capital One describes a Data Science Challenge: a take-home assignment that the candidate completes before the Power Day.
