## How Capital One describes its interviews {#overview}
source: https://www.capitalonecareers.com/what-to-expect-during-your-capital-one-interview-students-101

Capital One's careers site says candidates who complete the initial application steps are invited to a series of interviews called a Power Day. Every candidate has one or two job fit interviews, which look at aptitude for the role, the team and Capital One's culture. Behavioral questions, which usually begin with "Tell me about a time when...", are used for most positions.

## Case interviews {#case-interviews}
source: https://www.capitalonecareers.com/what-to-expect-during-your-capital-one-interview-students-101
source: https://www.capitalonecareers.com/4-tips-to-ace-your-capital-one-case-interview-101-students

Capital One says campus, business analyst, product manager and finance positions typically require a case or mini-case interview, in which the candidate works through a real-life business problem; a mini-case takes about 30 minutes. Its case interview tips say there is usually more than one right answer, the answer should be a recommendation with reasons, and candidates should explain their thinking, defend it with data, break down their approach in non-technical terms, and let their skills show through the case instead of talking about their resume.
