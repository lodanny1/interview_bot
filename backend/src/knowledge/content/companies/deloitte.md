## How Deloitte describes behavioral interviews {#overview}
source: https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/careers/join-deloitte/recruiting-tips/behavioral-interview-tips.html

Deloitte's US recruiting tips recommend structuring behavioral answers with the STAR method (situation, task, action, result), drawing on the experiences highlighted in the candidate's resume and the skills and competencies the position requires. A Deloitte recruiting specialist recommends doing mock interviews.

## Case and scenario interviews {#case-and-scenario}
levels: intern, entry
roles: consulting, business-analysis
source: https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/careers/join-deloitte/recruiting-tips/case-and-scenario-interview-tips.html

Deloitte US says entry-level Consultative Offerings candidates may have a case interview and entry-level Audit and Assurance candidates a scenario interview, which test problem solving, analytical ability, and strategic and logical thinking. Its suggested approach is to understand the issue with clarifying questions, identify assumptions, summarize the issues and findings, state recommendations, and outline next steps. A recruiter says the interview is not about getting the right answer but how well the candidate responds under pressure; Deloitte also advises taking notes, not using clarifying questions as a crutch, and sharing a framework and initial hypothesis.
