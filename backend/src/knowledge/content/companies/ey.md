## How EY describes its interviews {#overview}
source: https://www.ey.com/en_us/careers/interview-tips
source: https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-us/careers/documents/ey-interview-tips.pdf

EY US says it leans heavily on behavioral interviewing, which assumes past behavior predicts future performance and focuses on knowledge, skills and abilities backed by specific examples; it suggests preparing 10 to 15 examples that can be adapted to a question. Its interview guide says interviews are typically held by video, generally last 30 to 45 minutes, and are a behavioral interview, a case study interview or a combination. In a case study interview the candidate reads a sample business scenario and then discusses it with the interview facilitator.
