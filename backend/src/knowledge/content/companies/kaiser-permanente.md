## How Kaiser Permanente describes its hiring {#overview}
source: https://www.kaiserpermanentejobs.org/our-hiring-process

Kaiser Permanente says that for some positions it runs online pre-hire assessments covering reasoning ability, behavior-related traits, job skills such as typing, and subject knowledge. A talent acquisition professional may contact candidates for additional screening, which may lead to interviews by phone, in person and/or by video. Offers are contingent on pre-employment screenings and verifications, including drug and background checks.
