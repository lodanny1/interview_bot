## How UnitedHealth Group describes its interviews {#overview}
source: https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/careers/en/hiring-process/advice/interview-tips.html

UnitedHealth Group describes three interview formats: in person at an office, clinic or event; a live video conversation; and a digitally recorded interview with preset questions, up to 3 minutes per answer, a visible countdown and, in most cases, up to 3 re-recordings. It says all digital interviews are reviewed by a team member, not artificial intelligence. It advises learning about its mission, values and the broader healthcare industry, highlighting relevant skills, experiences and accomplishments, and preparing questions about the role, team and company.
