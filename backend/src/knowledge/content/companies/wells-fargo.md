## How Wells Fargo describes its interviews {#overview}
source: https://www.wellsfargojobs.com/en/life-at-wells-fargo/our-hiring-process/

Wells Fargo says its interview process varies by business group, and that interviews may be by phone, in a group, or one-on-one with a hiring manager or recruiter.

## Behavioral-based interviews {#behavioral}
tags: behavioral
source: https://www.wellsfargojobs.com/en/resources/behavioral-based-interview/

Wells Fargo describes behavioral-based interviews as focusing on past behaviors to predict future performance, and asks candidates to answer with the situation, behavior and outcome (SBO) method, which it says was formerly called the STAR method. It recommends researching Wells Fargo and the role, compiling and practicing answers to common behavioral questions, and sending a thank-you note to the hiring manager and panelists.
