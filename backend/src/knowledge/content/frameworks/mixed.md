## How mixed interviews work {#overview}

A mixed interview combines several question types in one session. It usually opens with background and motivation questions, moves between behavioral questions and role-specific or technical questions, and ends with time for the candidate's own questions. Interviewers use the mix to check that the candidate's stories and technical depth are consistent.

## Balancing a mixed session {#balance}

A balanced mixed session covers at least one behavioral question answered with a specific example, at least one question that tests role-specific skills, and one question about motivation for the role or company. Candidates do well when they switch styles cleanly: structured stories for behavioral questions, and step-by-step reasoning for technical ones.
