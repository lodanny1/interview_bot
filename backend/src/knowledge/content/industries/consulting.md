## Interviewing in consulting and professional services {#overview}

Consulting and professional services firms sell their people's expertise to clients, so interviewers often probe structured thinking, client-ready communication, teamwork, and how candidates handle ambiguity, travel or changing priorities. Many firms use case or scenario questions, and some business lines use technical or accounting questions instead.

## Questions to expect {#common-questions}

Expect questions about why the candidate wants client-facing work, a time they explained something complex to a non-expert, a time they worked on a team under a tight deadline, and how they would approach an unfamiliar industry or problem.
