## Interviewing in financial services {#overview}

Financial services employers work under regulation and handle sensitive customer and financial data, so interviewers often probe attention to detail, risk awareness, integrity and sound judgment alongside role skills. Many finance and business roles include quantitative or case-style questions, and technology roles in finance may ask about security and reliability.

## Questions to expect {#common-questions}

Expect questions about a time the candidate caught an error or managed a risk, how they handle confidential information, how they explain numbers to non-specialists, and why they want to work in financial services rather than another industry.
