## Interviewing in healthcare {#overview}

Healthcare employers serve patients and handle protected health information, so interviewers often probe patient focus, safety, privacy, reliability and teamwork across roles, including non-clinical ones. Clinical roles typically add scenario questions about patient care, and many employers screen for licenses or certifications early.

## Questions to expect {#common-questions}

Expect questions about why the candidate wants to work in healthcare, a time they put a patient's or customer's needs first, how they handle confidential information, how they respond to stressful or emotional situations, and how they work with people in different roles.
