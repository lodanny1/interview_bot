## Interviewing in technology {#overview}

Technology employers often run structured processes with separate rounds for role skills and behavioral questions, and many use coding, design or case exercises even for non-engineering roles. Interviewers tend to value comfort with fast change, learning new tools quickly, data-informed decisions and examples of shipping work in small iterations.

## Questions to expect {#common-questions}

Expect questions about the products the employer builds, how the candidate keeps their skills current, a time they worked with incomplete requirements, and how they would measure the success of a feature or project.
