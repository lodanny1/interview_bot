## What data interviews assess {#overview}

Data analyst and data scientist interviews usually assess the ability to turn a business question into an analysis, work with data using SQL and a language such as Python or R, apply statistics correctly, and explain findings to people who are not analysts. Many processes include a technical screen, a take-home or live analysis, and behavioral questions about past projects.

## Early-career expectations {#early-career}
levels: intern, entry

For interns and entry-level candidates, interviewers check fundamentals: writing SQL with joins, grouping and filtering, cleaning messy data, building a clear chart, and explaining basic statistics such as averages, distributions and correlation versus causation. Coursework, class projects and personal analyses are valid examples when the candidate can explain their own choices.

## Mid-level expectations {#mid-level}
levels: mid

Mid-level candidates are expected to own analyses from question to recommendation: choose the right metric, design or evaluate an experiment, spot data quality problems, and present results that changed a decision. Interviewers often ask how a past analysis was used and what the candidate did when the results were inconclusive.

## Senior and lead expectations {#senior-lead}
levels: senior, lead
tags: leadership

Senior and lead candidates are expected to define metrics and analytics strategy for a product or business area, set standards for data quality and experimentation, mentor analysts, and influence leaders who disagree with the data. Interviewers probe the business impact of their work and how they prioritized competing requests.

## Technical screens {#technical-screens}
tags: technical

Technical screens commonly include SQL questions on a small schema, interpreting a chart or experiment result, probability and statistics questions, and a case where the candidate investigates why a metric changed. Interviewers look for clarifying questions about how the data was collected and for sanity checks on results.
